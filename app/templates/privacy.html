Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-18

ShortBridge is a private, self-hosted tool built and operated by a single individual for their own personal use — migrating their own YouTube Shorts to their own TikTok account. It is not a public service, has no other users, and is not operated by a company. This page exists because third-party platforms (TikTok, Google) require a published privacy policy before an app can request access to an account, even a personal one.

What data this application stores

  • OAuth tokens for the YouTube and TikTok accounts you connect — encrypted at rest, used only to call those platforms' official APIs on your behalf (reading your YouTube channel's videos, and, once you enable it, posting to your TikTok account).
  • YouTube video metadata synced from your channel: titles, descriptions, durations, thumbnails, publish dates, and video IDs.
  • TikTok video metadata (once connected with the relevant scope): video IDs, titles, and publish dates, used only to avoid publishing the same content twice.
  • Video files, if and when you choose to download them into this application (manually, or via the optional home download agent described in the project's own documentation) — along with a hash and basic technical metadata (resolution, duration, codec) used to detect duplicates.
  • An activity log of actions this application takes (syncing, publishing, errors) for troubleshooting.

What this data is used for

Solely to operate the application as you've configured it: syncing your YouTube channel, matching video files to the right YouTube video, and publishing to your own TikTok account according to the schedule you set. Nothing here is used for advertising, analytics, or any purpose beyond running the tool for its owner.

Who can access this data

The single administrator account of this application (its owner/operator). There are no other user accounts. This application is not operated by a company, does not sell or share data with third parties, and does not use any analytics or tracking services.

Third-party services

This application calls the official YouTube Data API and TikTok APIs on your behalf, using the access you explicitly grant via OAuth. Your use of YouTube and TikTok themselves remains subject to their own privacy policies and terms.

How to disconnect / delete data

Disconnecting an account (from the Connections page) revokes the stored tokens with the provider and deletes them from this application. Because this is a self-hosted, single-operator tool, the operator has direct access to delete any or all stored data (video metadata, files, logs) at any time.

Contact

This project's source code and issue tracker: github.com/felipx1/shortbridge.
